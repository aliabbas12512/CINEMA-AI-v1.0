Shehzada Zain aur Jadui Chiragh

Bohat purane zamane ki baat hai. Pahaaron ke darmiyan Noorabad naam ki ek saltanat thi, jahan Crystal Palace ke sheeshe ke gumbad chandni mein chamakte thay.
Ek raat saltanat par andhera chha gaya. Shehzada Zain mehal ki purani library mein gaya aur wahan usay ek sunehra chiragh mila.
Zain ne chiragh ko haath mein liya aur aahista se kaha: "Yeh chiragh kaisa hai? Is mein ajeeb si garmi hai."
Achanak chiragh se neeli roshni nikli aur Pari Noor zahir hui. Us ke par chandni ki tarah chamak rahe thay.
Pari Noor ne muskura kar kaha: "Shehzade, main tumhari madad karungi. Lekin roshni sirf himmat se wapas aati hai."
Zain ne apni talwar uthayi aur kaha: "To phir chalo, Noorabad ko andheron se azaad karte hain."
Dono mehal ke sab se oonche minaar par gaye. Zain ne chiragh hawa mein buland kiya aur Pari Noor ne jadu padha.
Ek tez roshni saltanat par phail gayi, andhera ghayab ho gaya, aur Noorabad ke log khushi se naach uthe.
